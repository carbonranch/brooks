# Reference links for "Orthopteran Song 101326.docx" (checked 2026-10-15)

What was done. The reference list in the manuscript (`Orthopteran Song 101326.docx`, "References" section) and the
corrected list in `REFERENCES_2026-10-13.md` (sections A, B, D) were merged into one de-duplicated list of 69 entries,
with the 2026-10-13 corrections taking precedence (Alexander 1957a/b/c letters, Allard 1930a/b, Hayward volume 9,
Scudder 1868 not 1968, library-proxy URLs dropped). For each entry a link was sought, DOI first. Candidate DOIs came
from the Crossref API (`query.bibliographic`) and were accepted only when author, year, title, journal, volume and
pages in the Crossref record agreed with the entry; every DOI was then resolved through doi.org (handle lookup plus
landing page). Publisher sites that block automated fetches (OUP, Wiley, Science, Company of Biologists, University of
Chicago Press journals, AIP, PNAS, Canadian Science Publishing, PeerJ) returned HTTP 403 to the script, but in every
such case the doi.org handle resolved to the correct article URL (volume, first page and title slug in the URL) and the
Crossref record matched, so these are marked "DOI resolves". Items not in Crossref were traced to the OSU Knowledge
Bank, Internet Archive, Wikisource, BHL/BioStor, e-periodica, SINA, arXiv, DataCite and the University of Chicago Press.

Counts: 69 entries; 67 with a verified link (56 DOIs, 11 other verified pages); 1 not found (Riede 1993); 1 not
applicable (Eisen 2026, in preparation). Side findings worth acting on are marked **[note]**.
