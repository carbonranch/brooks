# Data and figure license

The tables in `data/`, the figures in `figures/` and the pages in `supplement/` are released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cite the paper and
this release (see `CITATION.cff`).

Third-party material is not redistributed here: the Singing Insects of North America recordings and pages
(https://orthsoc.org/sina/), the Walker & Funk (2014) supplementary tables, and iNaturalist recordings. The tables in
`data/reference_tests/` hold only our measurements of those recordings and their tests against the paper's lines.
